Frequently Asked Questions

Can the system be customized for our hospital workflows?

Yes, RoghanCare is highly customizable. We work with your clinical teams to map out specific escalation paths, role assignments, and department structures to fit your exact operational needs.

Does it work offline?

While primary notifications require an internet or cellular connection, our system supports SMS and Voice call fallbacks to ensure delivery even in low-bandwidth areas.

Is there a mobile app available?

Absolutely. We provide dedicated, secure mobile applications for both iOS and Android platforms, designed specifically for rapid response by medical staff on the go.

How fast are alerts delivered?

Alerts are dispatched in real-time, typically arriving within a fraction of a second. The system also includes an auto-escalation feature if an alert is not acknowledged within 10 seconds.
